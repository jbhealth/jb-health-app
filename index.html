One card per day. Add each leg as From → To with postcodes. Auto-fill miles looks up the postcodes and gets the driving distance for you (per-leg Auto, or all blank legs at once); the Maps button stays for checking or for legs without a postcode. Claim = miles × rate. Auto figures come from free postcode + routing services — reproducible and fine for records, but may differ slightly from Google, so spot-check anything that looks off. The postcodes are your HMRC route record. Not tax advice — confirm your rate basis with your accountant.
Amount (£) = amount × % business use. Net = income − expenses − mileage claim (pulled live from the Mileage tab). Export CSV for your accountant / Self Assessment.
